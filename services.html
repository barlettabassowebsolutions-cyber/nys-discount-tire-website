Auto Repair

Plenty can go wrong with a vehicle, and some problems put your safety at risk. We repair most makes and models, foreign and domestic — from a simple brake pad replacement to a complete engine overhaul. We'll do the job right the first time and get you back on the road.

Preventative Maintenance

Regular tune-ups keep your vehicle in good shape, but good maintenance goes beyond an oil change. Tire rotations, fluid changes, brakes, the transmission and much more all need attention on schedule. Don't wait for a breakdown — bring your vehicle in for preventative maintenance today!

Fleet & Diesel Service

Running a business with work vans or trucks? Our fleet services help keep your company vehicles on the road, and our diesel specialists know diesel trucks and engines inside and out. Call us to talk about service for your fleet.

Auto Body

You come first. When your vehicle arrives, we inspect it carefully and prepare a complete damage estimate you can give to your insurance company.

Once you approve the work, we take apart the damaged area and check the whole vehicle again for hidden damage. If we find more, we'll work with your insurance company to get the extra repairs approved.
